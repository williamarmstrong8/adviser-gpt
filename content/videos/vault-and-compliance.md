Your Vault is the foundation for compliant AI-assisted drafting. This video shows how to maintain it and work with compliance.

## What we cover

- What belongs in the Vault (and what doesn’t)
- How to tag and organize content for compliance review
- Updating the Vault when policies or language change
- How AdviserGPT uses the Vault so suggestions stay in scope

## Best practices

Review Vault content on a regular schedule. Keep disclosures and regulated language in one place so every draft starts from the same approved baseline.
