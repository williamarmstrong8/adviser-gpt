Your Vault is the single source of truth for approved language, Q&A pairs, and positioning. AdviserGPT uses it to suggest accurate, on-brand answers. Here’s how to get started.

## What to put in the Vault

Include your most frequently used content: standard DDQ answers, firm overviews, investment process descriptions, compliance disclosures, and any language that must stay consistent across RFPs and marketing.

## Upload and structure

Upload documents or paste content into the Vault. Organize by topic or questionnaire section so your team can find and update content easily. Tag content by use case (e.g. RFP, DDQ, consultant database) if that helps.

## Keeping it current

Assign an owner to review and update the Vault on a schedule (e.g. quarterly). When product or compliance language changes, update the Vault first so all new drafts reflect the latest approved wording.
