Every year we survey asset managers and wealth advisers on how they handle RFPs, DDQs, and consultant questionnaires. Here’s a snapshot of what we’re seeing in 2026.

## Volume and turnaround

RFP and DDQ volume continues to rise. The average firm reports more requests than the year before, with similar or smaller teams. Turnaround expectations from consultants and allocators have not relaxed.

## Where time goes

Most time is still spent on finding the right prior answer, tailoring it to the new question, and getting it through compliance. Firms that centralize content and use AI-assisted drafting report meaningful reductions in time-to-first-draft.
