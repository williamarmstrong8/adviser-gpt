Adopting AI for sales and marketing content doesn’t mean handing over the keys. A compliance-first strategy keeps your team in control while still gaining speed and consistency.

## Start with approved content

AdviserGPT is designed to work from a central Vault of approved language, disclosures, and positioning. The model suggests answers based on what you’ve already approved, so outputs are grounded in compliant material.

## Review workflows that scale

We recommend a clear workflow: draft with AI, then review with compliance before anything goes to clients. Many firms treat the Vault as the single source of truth so that every draft starts from the same baseline.

## Governance in practice

Define who can add or change Vault content, how often it’s updated, and how AI use is documented. With those guardrails, AI becomes a productivity tool that compliance can support rather than block.
